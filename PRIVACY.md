# Bond Privacy Policy

*Last updated: [DATE]. Replace [BRACKETS] before publishing.*

## The short version

Bond keeps your pet's information on your own phone. We don't have a copy.

## What stays on your device

Your pet's name, species, breed, date of birth, lifestyle setting, and collar
preferences are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted to us.
Deleting the app deletes them permanently. We cannot recover them for you.

## What we collect

**If you join the waitlist or place an order:** your email address, and for
orders your name and shipping address. We use these to contact you about your
order and, if you agreed, to send occasional updates. Payments are processed by
Stripe — we never see or store your card number.

## What we never do

- Sell or rent your information to anyone
- Show you advertising
- Track you across other apps or websites
- Collect location data
- Access your photos, contacts, microphone, or camera

## Notifications

Milestone reminders are scheduled on your device by your device. No server is
involved and no data leaves your phone to produce them.

## Bluetooth

If you own a Bond collar, the app uses Bluetooth to send it a short list of
upcoming dates and colours. That connection is local, between your phone and
your collar. Nothing is sent to us.

## Your rights

Email [privacy@yourdomain.com] to see, correct, or delete anything we hold. We
respond within 30 days. If you're in California, the EU, or the UK you have
additional statutory rights — the same address reaches us.

## Children

Bond isn't directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect their
information.
