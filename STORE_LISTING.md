## Description

> A golden retriever turns fifty somewhere in her fifth year. Nobody tells you.
>
> Bond works out where your pet actually is on their own timeline, then
> reminds you every time they reach somewhere worth marking.
>
> **Real science, not the seven-year myth**
> Multiplying by seven has no scientific basis. For dogs we use the 2020
> epigenetic clock from UC San Diego, built by reading DNA methylation in 104
> Labradors. For cats, the 2021 AAHA/AAFP veterinary life stage guidelines. For
> ten other species we model from published maturity and longevity data — and we
> label those clearly as estimates, because you deserve to know the difference.
>
> **Twelve species**
> Dogs (22 breeds, because a Great Dane and a Chihuahua age nothing alike), cats,
> rabbits, ferrets, guinea pigs, rats, chinchillas, hedgehogs, sugar gliders,
> opossums, raccoons, skunks.
>
> **The year ahead, not the years left**
> A dial showing the next twelve months and every celebration in it. We built
> this to make the time feel full, not to count anything down.
>
> **Milestones paced properly**
> A puppy ages about 57 human years in its first year; a senior dog about one.
> Bond adapts so you get roughly eight moments a year instead of thirty in
> year one and silence after that.
>
> **Every household**
> Add as many pets as you like. Switch between them in a tap.
>
> Free. No account. No ads. Your pet's information never leaves your phone.
